## Why federal

1. **Mission alignment.** The problems we're good at — orchestrating intelligent software over regulated data, with auditable money flows — map well to federal software modernization work.
2. **Durability.** Federal contracts reward the kind of engineering discipline we already practice (server-authoritative trust, migration-based schema evolution, deterministic control planes around non-determinism).
3. **Non-dilutive capital.** SBIR funding advances both the agency and the Gems family roadmap without diluting founders.
4. **Referenceable work.** A federal win de-risks future commercial and federal pursuits simultaneously.

## What a capability statement should contain (template)

When we draft or update our one-page capability statement, include:

1. **Cover line** — "PartyGem AI — AI/ML & full-stack consulting. Makers of PartyGem." + contact block.
2. **Core competencies** — short bullet list aligned to the themes above.
3. **Differentiators** — we ship a live product (PartyGem) that exercises our platform; we pair deterministic Go + SQL with AI rather than letting AI own the ledger; we're comfortable owning end-to-end engagements from Discovery through Handoff.
4. **Past performance** — PartyGem production alpha, NVIDIA Inception membership, relevant commercial engagements (named only with client permission).
5. **Corporate data** — UEI (from SAM.gov), NAICS codes (primary: `541511` Custom Computer Programming Services; also `541512`, `541519`, `541715`), CAGE code (when available), SBA size (small business), contact email `contact@partygemai.com`.
6. **Teaming posture** — explicit welcome of subcontracting and SBIR Phase-0/I partnerships.

Keep it to **one page**. Update it whenever SAM.gov status, NAICS coverage, or past performance materially changes.

## Engagement etiquette

- **Contracting officers:** Respond within one business day. State our SAM.gov renewal status up front. Never overclaim certifications we don't yet hold.
- **Prime contractors:** Be explicit about our small-business status and where we genuinely differentiate (AI/ML + full-stack + real product experience).
- **Solicitations:** Read the SOW carefully before responding. If something is out of scope for us (clearance required, very large staff ramp, pure hardware), say so and offer a narrower piece.
- **No secrets in email or chat.** Anything sensitive goes through approved channels per the agency in question.
