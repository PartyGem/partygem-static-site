# Company Overview

## What we are

**PartyGem AI** is a consulting agency specializing in **AI/ML** and **full-stack software development**. We take client engagements (private sector and U.S. federal) *and* build our own product line — the **Gems family** — in parallel.

We are a founder-led, technically heavy team. Our north-star thesis: the next decade of computing is about orchestrating specialized services with AI, and the best place to prove that thesis is in the messy, physical, people-first world of **events**.

## What we build

- **PartyGem** — our flagship product, an intelligent event management service. Live in alpha today.
- **Service Gem, Venue Gem, RoboGem, and future Gems** — additional products on the roadmap, each solving one sharp piece of the event economy.
- **Client software** — AI/ML and full-stack engagements for companies and (increasingly) federal agencies.

**PartyGem AI** — the company — is also the *orchestration layer* that links every Gem into one platform: identity, payments, entitlements, AI, and policy.

## How we're organized (today)

- **Co-founders:** Kevin Wang (CFO, business + GTM) and Syed Shah (Tech Lead, engineering + AI).
- **Operating mode:** Small, senior, high-trust; we prefer deeply-scoped engagements over hourly rentals.
- **Sectors:** AI/ML, full-stack SaaS, payments & commerce, events/hospitality, and — newly — federal software contracting.

## Programs & recognition

- **NVIDIA Inception Program** — member; provides us access to cutting-edge AI tooling and mentorship.
- **SAM.gov** — partnered with the U.S. federal government to bid on software-related contracts (registration is actively being renewed with updated tax documentation).
- **NSF SBIR** — pursuing non-dilutive research and productization funding.

## What we optimize for

1. **Shipped software over pitch decks.** PartyGem exists and runs real events.
2. **Server-authoritative trust.** Money, identity, and entitlements belong in the backend.
3. **Durable systems.** We design for the two-year version, not the demo.
4. **Good partners.** Both on the client side and in teaming arrangements for government work.
5. **Shared infrastructure.** Every engagement and every Gem feeds back into the platform.
